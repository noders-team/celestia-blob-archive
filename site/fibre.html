Fibre keeps data for four hours. The archive catches it in time.

Fibre payloads never enter the data square. A Pay-for-Fibre transaction on chain carries the payment promise and the validators’ signatures; the payload sits on validators’ Fibre servers and is deleted after 4 hours. The archive follows the chain head, downloads every payload while it is still there, checks it against the paid commitment and keeps it next to the promise.

What each capture keeps

The payload, byte for byte, stored once per blob ID. Next to it, a record with the raw transaction (payment promise and validator signatures), the block time, when it was captured and its status: archived, expired (gone before it could be fetched), unrecoverable (failed the check), withdrawn or skipped (over the daily cap).

A later stage adds the transaction’s share proof against the block’s data root and the validator set, so the quorum can be checked too.
