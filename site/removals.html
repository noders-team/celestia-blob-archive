Asking for data to be removed

Everything posted to Celestia is public, and the archive keeps it so it can still be read after the network forgets it. We remove an archived blob when there is a legitimate legal reason, and we list every removal publicly.

How to ask

abuse@noders.team

Include what identifies the blob and why it should go:

  • the network (Mainnet Beta or Mocha) and the block height;
  • the namespace and commitment, or the Fibre blob ID; the block page shows both;
  • the legal basis for the request and who is making it.

We answer every request. If we cannot act on it, we say why.

What a removal does

  1. The blob is added to the network’s tombstones.json with the date and reason. Entries are never taken off that list.
  2. Within a minute the read API stops serving it and answers “withdrawn” instead, so rollup clients look elsewhere rather than treat it as never posted.
  3. The blob’s record is overwritten in place with a marker of the same length. Its data, signer and proof are gone; every other blob and proof in the archive stays checkable.
  4. Copies in downstream caches expire within a day; op-alt-da fallback objects within 5 minutes.

The archive cannot remove anything from Celestia itself, or from other archives and nodes that hold the same block.
