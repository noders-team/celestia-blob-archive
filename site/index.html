Proof, not promises

Don’t trust us. Check it.

Every archived blob is stored with its share proof against the block’s data root. The block page runs the checks in your browser and tells you who checked each step.

  1. The bytes match what was paid for

    Your browser lays the blob out as 512-byte shares and recomputes its share commitment.

    Your browser
  2. The shares produce the row roots

    It hashes the shares into their rows of the data square, using the sibling hashes from the proof.

    Your browser
  3. The row roots are under the data root

    The archive checks this before it serves a blob; cba verify repeats it against a node you run.

    The archive, or your node
  4. The data root is in the signed block

    Your browser rebuilds the block hash from the header and compares it with the hash validators signed.

    Your browser
